# Story and sound direction

Noir follows Mara's ribbon into the sealed city. Regent Ilyan built a bell engine to hold his daughter Elian at the instant of death, sacrificing a living name each night. The city's ten hidden memories are witnesses to what the engine erased. Each chapter has an opening exchange, two environmental records, and an exit beat. Collecting all ten hidden memories opens the ending in which Noir speaks the witnesses together and breaks the bell. The other endings trade Noir's name for the survivors' passage or let Noir return home alone.

Version 0.3 uses three original sampled-instrument arrangements with synchronized encounter stems. They have written note sequences, instrumental changes, rhythmic sections and a recurring motif; ten quiet environmental beds preserve chapter atmosphere. Music and effects have independent controls. The score uses licensed GeneralUser GS instrument recordings, not music from the reference games. Effects remain synthesized sound design. See [the current production manifest, license and validation](AUDIO_MANIFEST.md).

Run `python scripts/compose_audio.py` with NumPy and Node to regenerate the bundled files. The script downloads pinned free production dependencies on its first run. There are six stereo 24 kHz score/stem files and twenty-seven mono 22.05 kHz environment/effect files, approximately 54.7 MB. All remain offline during play. Checked full-volume stacked mixes peak at 0.840 or lower; physical-device listening remains a separate review step.

The master defaults to 70% and existing custom volume/mute choices are retained. The earlier v0.2.1 gain fix is recorded in `audio-mix-021.json`; the new score's current mix validation is bundled with its production manifest. Numerical headroom does not establish musical quality or perceived loudness.
