# Story and sound direction

Noir follows Mara's ribbon into the sealed city. Regent Ilyan built a bell engine to hold his daughter Elian at the instant of death, sacrificing a living name each night. The city's ten hidden memories are witnesses to what the engine erased. Each chapter has an opening exchange, two environmental records, and an exit beat. Collecting all ten hidden memories opens the ending in which Noir speaks the witnesses together and breaks the bell. The other endings trade Noir's name for the survivors' passage or let Noir return home alone.

The original soundtrack is synthesized by `scripts/compose_audio.py`; it uses no samples, clips, or borrowed melodies. Each of the ten chapters has a distinct 40-second low drone and irregular distant events. The constant layer sits beneath movement and dialogue. Actions use short, physical sounds: a muted launch, rush of air, struck metal, stone impact, and a longer death fall. Small pickups and page turns are restrained. The director limits repeat cues and simultaneous effects, fades chapter changes, and puts a gentle limiter after the master gain.

Run `python scripts/compose_audio.py` with NumPy to regenerate the bundled WAV files. They are mono, 22,050 Hz, signed 16-bit PCM. The generated set contains ten beds and thirteen effects, about 18.3 MB total. Audio was checked numerically after generation: chapter RMS levels were 0.0406–0.0530, chapter peaks 0.1315–0.2084, and loop sample jumps 0.00067–0.00137 on a full-scale 1.0 scale. Effects peak at or below 0.476. Listening on the target devices remains a separate review step.

Version 0.2.1 fixes two compounding attenuations: the default master is 0.70 rather than 0.32, and the chapter bus reaches 1.10 rather than 0.42. Their combined ambience gain is 0.77 instead of 0.1344, about 15.16 dB louder before compression. Effects receive a 2.4 gain multiplier; voice/cooldown caps and the compressor remain. Existing custom volume and mute choices are retained. An OfflineAudioContext check rendered chapter ambience with four simultaneous loud effects at maximum master volume through the actual compressor settings; peak was 0.7633, below full scale. See `audio-mix-021.json`. This checks electrical headroom, not perceived loudness on physical speakers.
